Mevora Community Guidelines

Mevora is built for respectful connections. The full guidelines are in the app.

  • Be respectful in profiles, messages, and calls.
  • No harassment, hate speech, threats, or violence.
  • No spam, scams, or fake accounts.
  • No sexual exploitation or content involving minors.
  • Do not share others' private information without consent.
  • Report concerns from a profile or chat, or contact support in Settings.

Violations may lead to warnings, suspension, or permanent removal.
