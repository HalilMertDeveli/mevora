Mevora Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 23, 2026

This page provides the public Privacy Policy for the Mevora mobile app. The full, localized policy is also available inside the app under Settings → Help & Support → Privacy Policy.

What we collect

Depending on how you use Mevora, we may process account identifiers (email, phone, Google/Apple/Spotify sign-in data), profile information, photos, approximate location (with permission), likes/matches, messages and call metadata, notification/device tokens, preferences, optional Spotify music taste signals, and technical logs required to operate Firebase-backed infrastructure.

Why we use data

To authenticate you, show compatible people, deliver matches and messages, improve safety (block/report), provide support, process optional Boost purchases, and comply with legal obligations.

Storage and security

Data is primarily stored in Google Firebase (Firestore, Storage, Authentication, Cloud Functions). Messages may be end-to-end encrypted when both users have published encryption keys. We use access controls and security rules to limit access.

Retention and deletion

We retain data while your account is active. You can permanently delete your account in the app via Settings → Account → Delete Account. Some records may be retained where required by law or fraud prevention.
